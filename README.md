BuyAnything v0.8 – Colony Services Update

This update introduces the complete Colony Services system, bringing powerful new automation features to your colony while keeping everything fully configurable and balanced.

✨ New Features
Colony Services

The Merchant Terminal can now perform a variety of paid colony-wide services:

🧹 Clean Colony – Instantly cleans dirt, filth, and other messes across the map.
☠️ Remove Bodies – Safely disposes of all corpses.
🪨 Remove Chunks – Clears stone chunks from the colony.
📦 Auto Haul – Instantly hauls designated items to appropriate storage.
🌲 Harvest Trees – Cuts all designated trees immediately.
🌾 Harvest Crops – Harvests all mature crops with a single click.
⛏️ Mine Designated – Instantly mines all designated rocks and ores.
🏗️ Finish Construction – Completes all player construction projects instantly.
🔧 Repair Buildings – Fully repairs damaged player-owned buildings.
⚙️ New Settings

Every Colony Service now includes:

Individual enable/disable toggle
Adjustable silver cost
Integrated into the BuyAnything settings menu

The economy is now much more customizable, allowing you to tailor services to fit any playstyle—from balanced progression to creative mode.

🏗️ Finish Construction Improvements

The Finish Construction service has received significant improvements:

Supports blueprints and construction frames
Correctly preserves selected building materials (wood, steel, plasteel, stone blocks, etc.)
Completed buildings are automatically assigned to the player faction
Uses configurable service pricing
Improved stability and construction handling
🛠️ Fixes & Improvements
Improved settings menu organization
Fixed multiple construction-related edge cases
Improved service reliability
Fixed material handling during instant construction
Improved compatibility with different building types
General performance optimizations
Various bug fixes and code cleanup

Thank you for using BuyAnything! More features—including expanded purchasing, improved delivery options, and additional colony automation—are planned for future updates.
